# Marine Term Translations

Welcome to the Marine Term Translations project! 🌊

## About This Project

We're building a comprehensive, multilingual database of marine and oceanographic terminology. Our mission is to make scientific marine terms accessible across languages, supporting researchers, educators, and maritime professionals worldwide.

**What we do:**
- Translate semantic terms from authoritative marine vocabularies (NVS by BODC)
- Create standardized multilingual marine terminology databases
- Provide accessible translation tools and resources for the marine science community

## How You Can Contribute

Whether you're a marine scientist, translator, developer, or administrator, there's a place for you in our community:

- **Translators**: Help translate marine terms into your language and review existing translations
- **Developers**: Contribute to our platform's technical development and features  
- **Administrators**: Manage translation workflows and coordinate community efforts
